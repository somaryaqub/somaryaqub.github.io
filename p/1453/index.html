Our Values

The way we carry the work.

رَحْمَة

We are responsive & compassionate

We meet people where they are, with dignity & mercy, & we move when it matters most.

خِدْمَة

We are people-centred & growth minded

We build with the community, not merely for it, & we keep learning as we serve.

أَمَانَة

We are accountable, transparent, & serve justly

We hold what is entrusted to us as an amana, & we answer for it openly.

Our Guiding Question

How can we support the whole person to flourish in our community?

✸

The whole person includes mental, emotional, physical, familial, social, economic, and spiritual well-being, honouring the dignity, strengths, and hopes of every person.

✸

Flourishing means people & families belong, have stability, opportunity, and support to realize their potential, lead with confidence, and contribute positively to communities.

✸

Our community includes the many geographical, cultural, and faith-based networks that shape identity, nurture, and create opportunities for people to care for one another.
