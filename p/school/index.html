The need

Students don’t have the supports they need

Students are arriving with anxiety, stress, grief, family strain and questions about who they are. Many needs go unspoken until they surface as withdrawal, conflict or missed school, and the help that exists is often off site, in one language, or behind a waitlist.

Teachers and administrators are overwhelmed

Teachers are asked to notice, respond and refer on top of teaching. Administrators absorb whatever is left. Good people are stretched past what the role can hold, with little training or backup.

The resources available don’t stretch far enough

Budgets rarely cover the range of supports students need. Even when a school can fund one position, that person cannot cover every language, background or kind of need.

The one-person trap

Even when a school can bring in one person, the problem soon outgrows them.

A single hire is a real step forward, and then their limits become the school’s limits. One person can only be in one place, connect with certain students, speak certain languages and work within one scope of practice. Whatever they cannot reach, the school cannot reach either.

Some students are left out. If the counsellor mainly connects with the girls, the boys go unserved.

The cause is not at school. A counsellor can help a student talk it through, but income pressure or unstable housing at home falls outside their scope.

The offering

A team, present in the school.

Not another referral line, and not one person doing everything. IslamicFamily brings a team with a visible, ongoing presence, for roughly the cost of a single counsellor.

Reflects who is there

A team that can respond to the diversity of the school: different genders, languages, cultures and faiths, so every student can find someone they are comfortable with.

Builds trust

A steady presence that earns rapport with students, parents and teachers alike, so help is sought early rather than after a crisis.

Supports the family

When a student’s challenge is rooted at home, the team can respond, connecting families to income, food, housing, newcomer and counselling supports.

Visible and accountable

Clear presence on site, a point of contact for staff, and regular reporting, so the school can see the work and stand behind it.

What the team does

Train the trainer for teachers

Quarterly sessions that build teacher confidence in recognizing distress, responding well, and knowing when and how to refer.

Direct referrals

A clear, fast pathway from your school into IslamicFamily counselling and holistic supports, without families having to start from scratch.

Short-form content for families

Ready-to-share short pieces for parents and students on topics like stress, belonging and when to reach out. Built to be repurposed in newsletters, social posts, assemblies and parent nights.

One-to-one student support

Confidential, student-centred counselling on site, so help is available where students already are.

Peer support training

Training for students who want to support their friends, building a culture of care that extends beyond the counsellor’s door.

Group workshops

Sessions on topics such as stress, identity, healthy relationships and belonging, tailored to the age and needs of your students.

Gendered supports

Male and female counselling presence, so every student can reach someone they feel comfortable talking to.

Supported referrals for families

Warm, multilingual hand-offs for parents and caregivers into counselling, newcomer, financial and crisis supports.

On site

Flexible on-site services and programming delivered at schools by qualified, spiritually grounded professionals, with the ability to make direct referrals into a proven team.

How we start
1

Needs conversation

We meet with your principal and student support leads to understand your students, families and existing supports, and recommend a package.

2

Partnership agreement

A clear agreement sets out roles, confidentiality, consent practices, referral pathways and cost, consistent with your school’s policies and legal obligations.

3

Staff orientation

A practical session for teachers and school leaders on how the team works, how to refer, and how privacy is protected.

4

Our team joins the school

Practitioners become a familiar presence on the agreed days, coordinated with your counsellors, teachers and administrators.

5

Reporting and review

Regular, privacy-protective reporting on activities and outcomes. Services adapt as trust deepens and needs change.
