How to write a land acknowledging dua

A dua is a prayer, not a script. Speak to Allah about the land, the people who have cared for it, and what you are responsible for.

  • Turn toward God and set your intention. Approach the dua with sincerity (tawajjuh and niyyah). Your goal is to honour the land and its peoples, not just to read words.
  • Remember whose earth it is. Allah is the Creator and Owner of the earth. Acknowledging the land is a religious and ethical act, a reflection of your responsibility (amanah) and the duty to uphold justice.
  • Speak to Allah, not to an audience. Dua is an intimate conversation with your Creator. Use the names and attributes that fit your prayer: the Most Merciful, the Most Just, the Most Wise.
  • Make it specific. Find the treaty and nations where you are with the Know your land tool below, then bring in nature, heritage or local features.

A recipe

  1. Begin with gratitude and praise

    Thank Allah for your blessings and for the gathering. Reflect on the qualities of Allah and the guidance of the messengers that inspire you.

    “In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. All praise is due to Allah, the Creator of the heavens, the earth, and all nations within it.”

    Ask yourself: Which attributes of Allah feel most relevant to this gathering or land?

  2. Acknowledge the land and its peoples

    Name specific nations and communities, and think about your responsibilities: treaties, shared humanity and stewardship.

    Justice: “Ya Allah, grant justice and healing to those affected by historical and ongoing injustices, including the families of the Missing and Murdered Indigenous Women and Girls.”

    Stewardship: “Oh Lord, help us walk gently upon this earth as faithful stewards (khalifa), honouring the care of its original guardians and working in partnership with them.”

    Truth and reconciliation: “Ya Haqq (The Truth), guide us to the truth of this land’s history and help us work sincerely toward reconciliation and mutual respect.”

    Ask yourself: What does this land and its history call you to uphold in your words and actions? How can your actions bring ease or justice?

  3. Ask for blessing in your actions or gathering

    Seek Allah’s guidance in your work and your place in the community.

    Ask yourself: How can I be a source of light, care or sustenance today?

  4. Honour ancestors and heritage

    Remember the efforts of those who came before you, and connect with the Prophet ﷺ and his actions.

    “O Allah, bless the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and all who strive for justice and care for the earth.”

    Ask yourself: What wisdom or gifts have they left for me, and how can I carry them forward?

  5. Close with intention and gratitude

    End with praise and gratitude, and a general Āmīn.

    Ask yourself: What is my personal hope or intention for this moment?
