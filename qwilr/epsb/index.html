Recommendations for Anti-Racist Schooling:
​Enhancing Belonging, Equity & Learning Outcomes Through Inclusive Holidays

Overview

EPSB has committed to “providing a welcoming, inclusive, safe and healthy learning and working environment; one that respects diversity, commits to anti-racism, and fosters a sense of belonging throughout the Division.” [Anti-racism & Equity Policy]. To demonstrate this, meaningful action is necessary. 

Since 2020 a committed group of student leaders, parents & teachers has been working with EPSB (board & admin) on building a school calendar that reflects EPSB’s values of inclusion/belonging & the practical needs of students. This group has identified important holidays in consultation with the community (Bent Arrow Indigenous Healing Society, Beth Shalom Synagogue, Chinese Benevolent Association, Sangath Youth (Sikh), numerous mosques, IslamicFamily.ca) & also collected 2000+ parent & teacher signatures.

Milestones

Community Consultation & Support: 2000+ signatures for the Inclusive Holidays Petition 

Accommodating Student Count Days: The Government of Alberta (Min LaGrange) has officially stated it is willing to work with EPSB on student count days and the official opposition (MLA Hoffman) has also voiced support for inclusive holidays. 

ATA Support: 50+ Teachers have signed an open letter to the ATA showing their support of inclusive holidays. inclusion.paperform.co 

Support for Working Families: Edmonton City Council has directed city administration to research providing free rec centre access to families during inclusive holidays, thereby minimizing strain on families needing childcare. 

EPSB Multi-Faith Calendar: EPSB released a simple multi-faith calendar that makes it easy for educators and admin to plan around the high-holidays of Edmonton’s diverse communities. EPSB Multi-Faith Calendar

Other jurisdictions: Grasslands Public Schools district (Brooks, Alberta) has recognized Eid as a holiday.  Source: CBC



Actions

Action 1: Implement the 8 inclusive holidays suggested by community in the 2023/24 calendar & beyond 

Recognize the following as school holidays: Bandi Chhor Divas, Diwali, Eid-ul-Adha, Eid-ul-Fitr, Indigenous Peoples Day, Lunar New Year, Winter Solstice & Yom Kippur. These dates can be determined based on the methodology EPSB has used for its Multi-Faith Calendar.

Background: rootson6.ca/calendarfaq 

Action 2: Create a simple process for students & families to engage EPSB (at the school & district level) to express their needs on the calendar

Create a suggestion form for input on the calendar on the EPSB homepage. Report on feedback prior to determining the schedule. 

Action 3: Monitor & share data on absences to inform future calendars 

When possible EPSB should work to accommodate additional holidays based on requests from students/families, data on absences, teacher insights, and developments in other jurisdictions. 

Action 4: Create opportunities to improve student belonging and learning outcomes through the calendar 

Administration should work to identify partnerships, and learning opportunities with community organizations that complement the calendar.

Action 5: Equip educators with relevant materials  

EPSB should work to equip teachers with materials that celebrate Edmonton’s diversity that are vetted by educators with lived-experience & local community organizations.

eg. Toronto District School Board’s (TDSB) Islamic Heritage Month Resource Guide tdsb.on.ca/Portals/0/docs/IHMResourceGuidebook-UPDATES&REVISIONS.pdf



Next Steps & Contact

We look forward to supporting EPSB trustees and administration to take action in developing and implementing an inclusive calendar that supports the needs of families and children. Inclusive holidays are an essential step to advancing student belonging & learning outcomes. EPSB has an opportunity to meaningfully demonstrate their values and their commitment to anti-racism through their calendar.
